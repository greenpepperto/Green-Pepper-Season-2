## Tournament Classification

Green Pepper Season 2 is designed as an open-registration European competition, with VRS ranking used for direct Playoff qualification, roster matching and applicable seeding procedures.

The four direct Playoff places are awarded to the four highest-ranked eligible European VRS teams among accepted entrants, based on the official **2 November 2026 VRS snapshot**. Direct qualification is a ranking outcome within the open-registration event; it is not an invitation.

The tournament is intended to operate in accordance with applicable Valve Tournament Operating Requirements. Any VRS status, alignment or approval remains subject to the relevant Valve requirements and confirmation. This README does not itself guarantee Valve approval or VRS status.

Please read the complete [Rulebook](RULEBOOK.md) for binding eligibility and competition regulations.

## Roster and Eligibility

- The tournament is intended for teams competing in the **European region**.
- Unless the official event information states otherwise, each participating team's active roster must contain at least **three European players**.
- All players must use their own registered Steam and FACEIT accounts.
- FACEIT Anti-Cheat is mandatory for all official matches.
- Account sharing, impersonation, unauthorized stand-ins and multi-teaming are prohibited.
- Only players listed on the team's approved roster may participate.
- Tournament administration may request information or evidence to verify player identity, account ownership, roster status and eligibility.
- **Only one team per esports organization may participate.**
- No two teams may share three or more players from their applicable five-player reference rosters.

### Roster Continuity

Green Pepper uses a fixed five-player reference roster for roster-continuity checks. Teams must field at least **three of the five players** from their applicable reference roster for the entirety of every official match, including the Group Stage, Play-In and Playoffs.

**VRS-matched teams**
- The reference roster is the five-player roster matched to the team's official VRS roster in the applicable snapshot.
- At least three players from that VRS roster must be fielded in every match.
- At least three of those players must remain on the team's official tournament roster throughout the event.
- A team is considered VRS-matched when at least three of its five registered starting players match the same official VRS roster. Substitutes do not count towards initial matching.

**Other teams**
- The reference roster is the five original starting players submitted in the team's accepted registration.
- At least three of those original starters must be fielded in every match.
- Later roster additions do not become part of the original five-player reference roster.

The qualifying three players may differ between matches. Approved roster changes do not waive the three-of-five requirement.

### Roster Changes and Substitutions

- Roster changes require administration approval where specified.
- Registered substitutes may be used between matches, provided all eligibility and roster-continuity requirements are met.
- During the Playoffs, teams may make no more than **two substitute-slot roster changes in total**, limited to the Sub 1 / Sub 2 slots and subject to administration approval.
- Replacing one player in either substitute slot counts as one change; replacing both substitute-slot players counts as two changes.
- Using an already registered substitute does not itself count as a substitute-slot roster change.
- A registered coach may substitute as a player only if listed on the official roster before the match, eligible to participate and the lineup continues to satisfy the applicable three-of-five requirement.

The [Rulebook](RULEBOOK.md) contains the full roster, eligibility and substitution regulations.

## Tournament Format

Green Pepper Season 2 features **24 teams** and uses four competition stages before crowning the champion.

| Stage | Teams | Format |
|---|---:|---|
| VRS Direct Qualification | 4 | Direct to Playoffs · Seeds 1–4 |
| Group Stage | 20 | 5 Groups of 4 · GSL · BO3 |
| Play-In | 4 | Single Elimination · BO3 |
| Playoffs | 16 | Double Elimination · BO3 |
| Grand Final | 2 | BO5 |

### VRS Direct Qualification

The four highest-ranked eligible teams in the official **European VRS snapshot dated 2 November 2026** qualify directly for the 16-team Playoffs.

- VRS-qualified teams do not participate in the Group Stage.
- The four teams occupy Playoff seeds 1–4.
- The highest-ranked eligible team receives seed 1.
- Eligibility is subject to applicable Valve requirements and the tournament's published rules.
- Direct qualification is based on published ranking criteria among accepted entrants and is not an invitation.

### Group Stage — GSL BO3

The remaining **20 teams** are divided into **five groups of four teams**, designated Groups A, B, C, D and E.

Each group follows a standard four-team GSL double-elimination format. **Every Group Stage match is BO3.** Each group consists of five matches:

| Match | Fixture | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Opening Match 1 | Group seed 1 vs Group seed 4 | Winner advances |
| Opening Match 2 | Group seed 2 vs Group seed 3 | Winner advances |
| Winners' Match | Winners of the opening matches | Winner qualifies 1st |
| Elimination Match | Losers of the opening matches | Loser finishes 4th and is eliminated |
| Decider Match | Loser of Winners' Match vs winner of Elimination Match | Winner qualifies 2nd; loser finishes 3rd |

Group qualification:
- **1st and 2nd place** in each group qualify directly for the Playoffs.
- **3rd place** in each group enters the cross-group ranking procedure for the Play-In.
- **4th place** in each group is eliminated.

Group seeding and the draw procedure will be published before the Group Stage.

### Third-Place Tiebreak

The five third-place teams are ranked using the following criteria, in order:

1. **European VRS ranking** in the official event snapshot.
2. Group Stage series record — more wins, then fewer losses.
3. Map differential.
4. Map win percentage.
5. Round differential.
6. Round win percentage.
7. Original Group Stage draw seed.
8. Organizer-administered random draw.

Teams without a published European VRS ranking are treated as unranked for the first criterion and are compared using the remaining criteria. The same announced VRS snapshot is used consistently for all five third-place teams.

The **four highest-ranked third-place teams** advance to the Play-In. The fifth-ranked team is eliminated.

### Play-In — Single Elimination BO3

The four eligible third-place teams compete in two BO3 semi-finals:

| Match | Fixture |
|---|---|
| Semi-final 1 | 1st-ranked third-place team vs 4th-ranked third-place team |
| Semi-final 2 | 2nd-ranked third-place team vs 3rd-ranked third-place team |

The two winners qualify for the 16-team Playoffs and receive seeds 15–16, ordered by their third-place ranking. The two losing teams are eliminated.

### Playoffs — 16-Team Double Elimination

The Playoffs feature:
- 4 direct VRS qualifiers.
- 5 Group Stage winners.
- 5 Group Stage runners-up.
- 2 Play-In winners.

All Upper Bracket and Lower Bracket matches are **BO3**. The Grand Final is **BO5**.

A team is eliminated after its second Playoff series loss. The Grand Final is contested between the Upper Bracket winner and the Lower Bracket winner. Whether a bracket reset or another Grand Final advantage applies will be announced before the Playoffs begin.

#### Playoff Seeding

| Seeds | Qualification |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | VRS direct qualifiers, ordered by European VRS ranking |
| 5–9 | Five Group Stage winners |
| 10–14 | Five Group Stage runners-up |
| 15–16 | Play-In winners, ordered by third-place ranking |

The Organizer will publish the consistent ordering method for Group Stage winners and runners-up, together with the complete Playoff bracket, before the first Playoff match.

## Match Integrity and Administration

Green Pepper expects all participants to compete fairly and professionally.

### Integrity Webcams

Every player participating in official matches must use an integrity webcam, unless administration has expressly approved a written exception before the match. Players must follow the official camera setup, framing and verification instructions. Deliberately disabling, obstructing, spoofing or manipulating a webcam feed, or refusing a reasonable integrity check, may result in a forfeit, disqualification or further sanctions.

### Prohibited Conduct

Prohibited conduct includes, but is not limited to:

- Cheating or using unauthorized third-party software.
- Sharing accounts, impersonation or using unauthorized stand-ins.
- Stream sniping or receiving outside information during a match.
- Exploiting game bugs or prohibited map positions to gain an unfair advantage.
- Match-fixing, collusion or intentionally manipulating results.
- Betting on own matches or using confidential tournament information for betting.
- Harassment, threats, discrimination or intentional match disruption.
- Refusing to follow reasonable instructions from tournament administrators.

Teams should preserve relevant demos, screenshots, recordings and FACEIT match information if an incident or protest occurs. Formal protests must be submitted through the official support channel, normally within **24 hours after a match concludes**, unless a different deadline is announced for a specific stage.

Tournament administration may investigate incidents, request evidence, correct results and apply proportionate penalties to protect competitive integrity.
