## 3. Tournament Format and Stage Structure

Green Pepper Season 2 features 24 teams and follows the Swiss Stage and Playoffs structure:

| Stage | Teams | Format | Qualification |
|---|---:|---|---|
| VRS Direct Qualification | 8 | Direct to Playoffs | Seeds 1–8 |
| Swiss Stage | 16 | Swiss System · BO3 | 3 wins qualify; 3 losses eliminate |
| Playoffs | 16 | Single Elimination · BO3 | One series loss eliminates |
| Grand Final | 2 | BO5 | Tournament champion decided |

### 3.1 VRS Direct Playoff Qualification

- Eight highest-ranked eligible teams in the official European VRS snapshot qualify directly for the Playoffs.
- The snapshot date used for Season 2 is **2 November 2026**.
- Direct qualifiers occupy Playoff seeds **1–8**, ordered by their European VRS ranking.
- Direct qualifiers do not participate in the Swiss Stage.
- Teams must still complete registration, payment, roster and eligibility requirements.
- Direct qualification is based on published ranking criteria among accepted entrants; it is not an invitation.

### 3.2 Swiss Stage — BO3

The remaining **16 teams** compete in a Swiss Stage.

- Teams are paired against opponents with the same current win-loss record.
- A team qualifies for the Playoffs after **three match wins**.
- A team is eliminated after **three match losses**.
- Every Swiss match is **BO3**, including advancement and elimination matches.
- Teams must not play the same opponent twice where a valid alternative pairing is available.
- Eight teams qualify from the Swiss Stage and join the eight direct VRS qualifiers in the Round of 16.

After Round 1, teams are paired within their current win-loss record group. Pairings follow the Rulebook and avoid rematches where a valid alternative is available.

### 3.3 Swiss Qualification and Playoff Seeding

The eight Swiss qualifiers are ranked in this order:

1. Qualification path: teams qualifying 3-0 rank ahead of 3-1 teams, who rank ahead of 3-2 teams.
2. Initial Swiss Stage seed.

The eight Swiss qualifiers receive Playoff seeds **9–16**, with the highest-ranked Swiss qualifier receiving seed 9. The eight VRS direct qualifiers occupy seeds **1–8**.

### 3.4 Playoffs — 16-Team Single Elimination

The Playoffs consist of eight VRS direct qualifiers and eight Swiss Stage qualifiers.

- The bracket is **Single Elimination**.
- All Playoff matches are **BO3**, except the Grand Final, which is **BO5**.
- A team is eliminated after one Playoff series loss.
- There is no third-place decider match.

## 5. Online Match Scheduling Guidelines

### Match Times and Availability

- Teams must be ready in their designated FACEIT matchroom at the confirmed start time.
- All eligible players must be available, using the approved roster and with FACEIT Anti-Cheat operational.
- Captains must remain reachable through the official tournament communication channel.
- Teams are responsible for checking the latest official schedule and match-specific instructions.
- There is no venue check-in and no requirement to attend a physical location.
- Teams are responsible for having a stable internet connection, working equipment, required game updates and access to FACEIT.

### Readiness and Grace Period

- The Rulebook's **15-minute grace period** applies unless the official schedule or a match administrator states otherwise.
- A team that is not ready after the grace period may receive a forfeit or other sanction under the Rulebook.
- Players must use the registered and approved roster and satisfy all roster-continuity requirements.

### Platform and Anti-Cheat

- All matches must be played on **FACEIT** using the matchroom and server arrangements designated by the Organizer.
- **FACEIT Anti-Cheat is mandatory** for every player participating in an official match.
- Players must use their own registered accounts. Account sharing and impersonation are prohibited.
- Technical, account or anti-cheat issues must be reported promptly to a tournament administrator through the official support channel.

### Results, Evidence and Disputes

- Captains must verify that the final result is correctly recorded on FACEIT.
- Score discrepancies, server issues and suspected rule violations must be reported promptly.
- Teams should retain relevant evidence, including screenshots, matchroom details, demos, recordings and error messages.
- Results and rulings remain subject to verification by tournament administration.
- Formal protests must follow the procedure and deadline in the Rulebook. The standard deadline is 24 hours after a match concludes unless a different deadline is announced.

### Rescheduling and Delays

- A match is not rescheduled until the opponent has confirmed the change and administration has approved it where required.
- Rescheduling must remain within the relevant stage's published window and must not compromise bracket integrity.
- Teams must not independently delay a match beyond its assigned window.
- Technical pauses, disconnections, replays and server problems are handled under the Rulebook and administrator instructions. A replay or reschedule is not automatic.
