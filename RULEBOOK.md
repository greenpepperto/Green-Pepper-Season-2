# Green Pepper Season 2 — Tournament Rules and Regulations

**Game:** Counter-Strike 2 (CS2)  
**Region:** Europe  
**Teams:** 24  
**Format:** 8 VRS Direct Qualifiers + 16-Team Swiss Stage + 16-Team Single-Elimination Playoffs  
**Match formats:** Swiss Stage BO3 · Playoffs BO3 · Grand Final BO5  
**Platform:** FACEIT  
**Entry Fee:** €250 per team  
**Prize Pool:** €5,000  
**VRS Snapshot:** 2 November 2026  
**Map Pool:** de_ancient · de_anubis · de_cache · de_dust2 · de_inferno · de_mirage · de_nuke  
**Dates and registration window:** To be announced.

This Rulebook carries forward the core roster, integrity, online-match, broadcast, conduct and administration principles used for Green Pepper Season 1, adapted to the Green Pepper Season 2 format. Green Pepper Season 2 retains the Season 1 entry fee, prize pool, FACEIT competition platform, VRS snapshot date and map pool. Season 2 dates and registration window will be announced separately.

## 1. General Information and Registration

### 1.1 Tournament Information

1.1.1. Green Pepper Season 2 is a European Counter-Strike 2 tournament organized by Green Pepper.

1.1.2. The tournament is intended to operate in accordance with applicable Valve Tournament Operating Requirements and other applicable rules. Any VRS status, alignment or approval is subject to the relevant Valve requirements and confirmation; this Rulebook does not itself guarantee VRS status or Valve approval.

1.1.3. The tournament is designed for European teams. Unless the official event information states otherwise, each participating team's active roster must contain at least three European players.

1.1.4. The tournament will feature 24 accepted teams, subject to the registration and eligibility process published by Green Pepper.

1.1.5. The official competition platform is **FACEIT**. All official matches, match rooms, results and standings will use the Green Pepper Season 2 FACEIT tournament infrastructure.

1.1.6. The official VRS ranking reference for Season 2 is the **2 November 2026 European VRS snapshot**. This snapshot is used for direct Playoff qualification, VRS roster matching and any other ranking procedure expressly stated in this Rulebook. VRS status and alignment remain subject to applicable Valve requirements and confirmation.

### 1.2 Registration

1.2.1. Teams must register through the official Green Pepper registration process.

1.2.2. Registration is complete only when all required team, player and contact details have been submitted and the Organizer has confirmed acceptance.

1.2.3. Each team must designate a captain during registration. Captain changes must be reported to administration, and the replacement captain must be listed on the approved roster.

1.2.4. Teams must provide accurate team names, organization and affiliation details, player identities, Steam account details and relevant competitive-platform profiles.

1.2.5. False, incomplete or misleading registration information may result in rejection, loss of a place, a match forfeit or disqualification.

1.2.6. Green Pepper may limit registration to 24 accepted teams and may operate a Waiting List. Registration deadlines, payment confirmation and Waiting List procedures will be announced separately.

1.2.7. If a place becomes available before the first official match, the Organizer may offer it to an eligible Waiting List team. A place is not guaranteed until the replacement has been formally confirmed. Replacement offers follow the VRS and HLTV ranking priority in Section 1.2.15. A replacement team has 24 hours from notification to complete registration and payment; otherwise, the offer expires and the next eligible team may be contacted.

1.2.8. **Open Registration and opening date:** Green Pepper Season 2 will use an open-registration process. The registration opening date and time will be announced through the official Green Pepper communication channels.

1.2.9. **Initial selection and contact:** On the date and time announced by the Organizer, Green Pepper will conduct an initial selection and contact process, aiming to contact up to **20 teams** from Open Registration. Priority will be given to the highest-ranked eligible VRS teams using the official VRS publication specified in Section 1.1.

1.2.10. If fewer than 20 eligible VRS-ranked teams have registered by the initial selection, all registered eligible VRS-ranked teams will be contacted first. Any remaining contacts, up to a maximum of 20 teams, will be allocated to non-VRS-ranked teams in order of completed registration.

1.2.11. If fewer than 20 teams in total have registered by the announced initial selection date and time, all registered teams will be contacted.

1.2.12. **Capacity expansion:** The Organizer reserves the right to expand the tournament capacity to a maximum of **24 teams**, depending on registration demand and organizational capacity. All participating teams acknowledge that the tournament may be expanded beyond its initially advertised capacity. Any expansion will be officially communicated through Green Pepper's official communication channels.

1.2.13. **Further selection and available places:** Following the initial selection, if tournament places remain available, priority will continue to be given to eligible VRS-matched teams. If no eligible VRS-matched team is available for a place, priority will pass to the highest-ranked eligible non-VRS team in the **HLTV World Ranking dated 2 November 2026**. If no eligible candidate has an HLTV ranking, the Organizer may use the average FACEIT Elo of the five registered starting players, followed by the earlier completed registration as the final tie-break.

1.2.14. **24-hour registration and payment deadline:** A team contacted and offered a place has **24 hours from the time of the Organizer's notification** to submit all required registration information and complete the full **€250 team entry-fee payment**. The place is provisional during this period. If the team fails to complete registration and payment within 24 hours, the offer expires automatically and the place may be offered to the next eligible team according to the priority order in this Rulebook. Registration is complete only after all required details and payment have been received and confirmed.
1.2.15. **Registration priority:** Eligible VRS-matched teams have first priority when Green Pepper reviews registrations, selects teams, confirms available places or fills vacancies. VRS-matched teams are ordered by their position in the official European VRS snapshot dated **2 November 2026**, with the higher-ranked team receiving priority. If no eligible VRS-matched team is available for the place or selection round, the highest-ranked eligible non-VRS team in the **HLTV World Ranking dated 2 November 2026** receives priority. If two or more eligible teams have the same applicable ranking position, or their order cannot be distinguished, the earlier completed registration takes priority. If no eligible non-VRS candidate has an HLTV ranking, the Organizer may use the average FACEIT Elo of the five registered starting players, with registration time as the final tie-break. Registration time alone does not give a non-VRS team priority over an eligible VRS-matched team. All teams remain subject to eligibility, roster, payment, capacity and applicable Valve requirements.

1.2.15. **Age requirement:** Only individuals **over 13 years of age** may register or participate in the tournament.

1.2.16. **Liability statement:** The captain of every participating team must sign the Organizer's liability statement covering equipment damaged as a result of misuse, negligence or theft. The signed statement must be submitted in the manner and by the deadline communicated by the Organizer.


### 1.3 Entry Fees and Refunds

1.3.1. The entry fee is **€250 per team**. Payment instructions and deadlines will be communicated through the official Green Pepper channels.

1.3.2. Registration is complete only after all required details have been submitted and the team's payment has been confirmed.

1.3.3. Once a team's registration payment has been confirmed, the entry fee is **non-refundable if the team chooses to withdraw**, regardless of the reason for withdrawal.

1.3.4. Registration fees will be refunded in full to all teams if Green Pepper Season 2 is cancelled by the Organizer, does not reach the required **24 accepted teams**, or does not obtain the intended **VRS status/alignment** required to proceed as advertised. Any one of these conditions is sufficient to trigger the full refund guarantee.

1.3.5. No other circumstances, including a team's voluntary withdrawal or inability to participate, entitle that team to a refund, subject to applicable law.

## 2. Rosters, Eligibility and Core Policy

### 2.1 Roster Submission

2.1.1. Each team must register five starting players. The permitted roster size, substitute slots and registration procedure will be stated in the official registration instructions. Teams must not field unregistered players.

2.1.2. A coach may substitute as a player only if listed on the official roster before the match, eligible to participate, and the lineup continues to satisfy the applicable three-of-five requirement.

2.1.3. Teams must submit each player's display name, Steam account details and competitive-platform profile requested by the Organizer.

2.1.4. Only players listed on the team's approved roster may participate in official matches.

2.1.5. Each player may represent only one team during the tournament. Multi-accounting, account sharing, impersonation and playing for multiple teams are prohibited.

2.1.6. Players must use their own accounts and must not allow another person to play on their behalf.

2.1.7. Administration may request reasonable evidence to verify a player's identity, account ownership, eligibility, roster status or VRS match.

### 2.2 Fixed Five-Player Reference Roster

2.2.1. A fixed five-player reference roster will be used for roster-continuity checks:

- **VRS-matched teams:** the five players on the team's matched official VRS roster in the applicable VRS snapshot used for Season 2.
- **Other teams:** the five original starting players submitted in the team's accepted registration.

2.2.2. Every team must field at least **three of the five players** from its applicable reference roster for the entirety of every official match, including the Swiss Stage and Playoffs.

2.2.3. The qualifying three players may differ from match to match. Teams are not required to nominate the same three core players for every match.

2.2.4. For a team to be considered VRS-matched, at least three of its five registered starting players must match the same official five-player VRS roster in the applicable snapshot. Substitutes do not count towards initial VRS matching.

2.2.5. A VRS-matched team must maintain at least three players from its matched VRS reference roster on its official tournament roster throughout the event. Falling below this threshold may result in disqualification or loss of VRS qualification, subject to applicable Valve requirements.

2.2.6. Approved roster changes do not waive the three-of-five requirement.

### 2.3 Duplicate Cores and Organizational Restrictions

2.3.1. No two teams may use a shared core of three or more players from their applicable five-player reference rosters.

2.3.2. **Only one team per esports organization may participate in Green Pepper Season 2.** This applies to teams belonging to, operated by, managed by, controlled by or officially representing the same organization, even if they use different names, rosters, managers or player accounts.

2.3.3. Each team must disclose its organization, ownership or control relationships and any other team with which it is affiliated.

2.3.4. Where affiliation is unclear, administration may assess ownership, management, contracts, shared staff, official representation and operational control. Shared sponsors or a shared service provider alone do not automatically establish that two teams belong to the same organization.

2.3.5. If multiple teams from the same organization register, administration may require the organization to nominate one eligible team. If a conflict is discovered after acceptance or after competition begins, administration may reject or disqualify the affected team or teams, amend results where necessary and apply further sanctions if information was concealed or misrepresented.

2.3.6. These restrictions are intended to prevent conflicts of interest, collusion, information sharing and other risks to competitive integrity.

### 2.4 Roster Changes and Substitutions

2.4.1. Once a team has submitted its roster, a maximum of **two player changes in total** will be permitted across the tournament. Each replacement of a player on the submitted roster counts as one change. Any change requires prior administration approval.

2.4.2. **Up to and including 16 days before the tournament starts:** teams may replace any player on their submitted roster, subject to the two-change limit and administration approval.

2.4.3. **From 15 days until 7 days before the tournament starts:** roster changes may still be requested, but the team's applicable VRS core must remain unchanged. For VRS-matched teams, this means the required three-player VRS core must be preserved; all teams must continue to satisfy the fixed five-player reference-roster and three-of-five requirements.

2.4.4. **Within 7 days of the tournament start:** roster changes are permitted only as emergency substitutions in exceptional circumstances. The request must be supported by valid and appropriate justification and is subject to explicit administration approval. An emergency substitution must never affect the applicable VRS core or reduce the team below the required three-of-five reference-roster threshold.

2.4.5. The two-change limit applies to all player replacements, including changes involving starting players or substitutes. Using a substitute who was already listed on the team's submitted roster does not count as a roster change.

2.4.6. No approved roster change alters the team's fixed five-player reference roster or waives any VRS, eligibility or roster-continuity requirement.

2.4.7. Teams may use an approved, registered substitute between matches, provided the lineup meets all eligibility and roster-continuity requirements. A substitution during a match or between maps is permitted only in a verifiable emergency and requires administration approval where circumstances allow. The captain must contact administration through the official support channel as soon as an emergency substitution is required. Administration may deny a substitution if it violates roster rules, materially disrupts the match or undermines competitive integrity.

## 3. Tournament Format and Structure

### 3.1 Tournament Overview

Green Pepper Season 2 features **24 teams**:

| Qualification route | Teams |
|---|---:|
| VRS Direct Qualification | 8 |
| Swiss Stage Qualification | 8 |
| **Playoffs total** | **16** |

### 3.2 VRS Direct Qualification

3.2.1. The eight highest-ranked eligible registered teams in the official **European VRS ranking**, using the **2 November 2026 snapshot**, receive direct qualification to the Playoffs.

3.2.2. VRS-qualified teams do not participate in the Swiss Stage.

3.2.3. The eight VRS teams occupy Playoffs seeds **1–8**, ordered by their position in the applicable European VRS snapshot. The highest-ranked team receives seed 1.

3.2.4. A team's eligibility and ranking status are subject to applicable Valve rules and the tournament's published eligibility requirements.

3.2.5. If a directly qualified team withdraws or becomes ineligible, the Organizer will determine a replacement under the published replacement policy and applicable Valve requirements.

3.2.6. Direct qualification is determined by the published ranking criteria and the event's open-registration process; it is not an invitation to participate.

### 3.3 Swiss Stage — BO3

3.3.1. The Swiss Stage consists of **16 teams**: the accepted and eligible teams that do not receive one of the eight direct Playoff places under Section 3.2.

3.3.2. The Swiss Stage uses a Swiss system. Teams are paired against opponents with the same current win-loss record.

3.3.3. A team qualifies for the Playoffs after **three match wins**.

3.3.4. A team is eliminated after **three match losses**.

3.3.5. Every Swiss match is **Best-of-Three (BO3)**, including all advancement and elimination matches.

3.3.6. Teams must not play the same opponent twice in the Swiss Stage where a valid alternative pairing is available.

3.3.7. Eight teams qualify from the Swiss Stage and join the eight teams directly qualified to the Playoffs in the Round of 16.

3.3.8. Swiss pairings are determined by the current Swiss seed, match record and the pairing rules in Section 4.

3.3.9. Administration may make a necessary pairing adjustment to prevent a rematch, resolve an unavoidable bracket conflict or preserve competitive integrity. Any adjustment must be applied consistently.

#### Initial Swiss Match-ups

Swiss seeds 1–16 are assigned to the 16 teams competing in the Swiss Stage, in order of their pre-event seed after the eight direct Playoff qualifiers have been removed. The first-round match-ups are:

| Swiss Round 1 |
|:--|
| 1 vs. 9 |
| 2 vs. 10 |
| 3 vs. 11 |
| 4 vs. 12 |
| 5 vs. 13 |
| 6 vs. 14 |
| 7 vs. 15 |
| 8 vs. 16 |

### 3.4 Playoffs — 16-Team Single Elimination

3.4.1. The Playoffs consist of **16 teams**: eight VRS direct qualifiers and eight Swiss Stage qualifiers.

3.4.2. The Playoffs use a **Single-Elimination bracket**. A team is eliminated after one Playoff series loss.

3.4.3. All Playoff matches are **Best-of-Three (BO3)**, excluding the Grand Final, which is **Best-of-Five (BO5)**.

3.4.4. The Round of 16 opening matches are arranged as follows:

| Round of 16 Match | Match-up | Quarter-final path |
|:--|:--|:--|
| R16-1 | Seed 1 vs. Seed 16 | Quarter-Final 1 |
| R16-2 | Seed 4 vs. Seed 13 | Quarter-Final 1 |
| R16-3 | Seed 5 vs. Seed 12 | Quarter-Final 2 |
| R16-4 | Seed 8 vs. Seed 9 | Quarter-Final 2 |
| R16-5 | Seed 7 vs. Seed 10 | Quarter-Final 3 |
| R16-6 | Seed 6 vs. Seed 11 | Quarter-Final 3 |
| R16-7 | Seed 3 vs. Seed 14 | Quarter-Final 4 |
| R16-8 | Seed 2 vs. Seed 15 | Quarter-Final 4 |

3.4.5. The bracket paths preserve the published seed structure. The two highest-seeded teams cannot meet before the Grand Final. There is no third-place decider match.

3.4.6. Quarter-final winners advance to the semi-finals according to the published bracket, and the semi-final winners meet in the Grand Final.

3.4.7. No team may be reseeded after the bracket has started except to correct a documented administrative error.

3.4.8. The Playoffs remain subject to the roster-change limits and three-of-five reference-roster rule in Section 2.

### 3.5 Playoffs Seeding

3.5.1. The initial seeding structure is:

| Seeds | Qualification |
|---|---|
| 1–8 | VRS direct qualifiers, ordered by European VRS ranking |
| 9–16 | Swiss Stage qualifiers, ordered by Swiss qualification ranking |

3.5.2. Swiss qualifiers are ranked using the criteria in Section 4.2: qualification path (3-0, then 3-1, then 3-2), followed by initial Swiss Stage seed.

3.5.3. The complete Playoffs bracket and all seeds will be published before the first Playoff match.

## 4. Swiss Qualification and Playoffs Seeding

### 4.1 Swiss Stage Pairing Rules

4.1.1. After the initial round, teams are grouped by their current win-loss record.

4.1.2. In Rounds 2 and 3, the highest-seeded team faces the lowest-seeded available opponent with the same record, provided the pairing does not create a rematch.

4.1.3. For later rounds, when a record group contains six teams, administration selects the first pairing row below that does not create a rematch. Teams within the group are ordered by current seed.

| Priority | Match 1 | Match 2 | Match 3 |
|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| 1 | 1 vs. 6 | 2 vs. 5 | 3 vs. 4 |
| 2 | 1 vs. 6 | 2 vs. 4 | 3 vs. 5 |
| 3 | 1 vs. 5 | 2 vs. 6 | 3 vs. 4 |
| 4 | 1 vs. 5 | 2 vs. 4 | 3 vs. 6 |
| 5 | 1 vs. 4 | 2 vs. 6 | 3 vs. 5 |
| 6 | 1 vs. 4 | 2 vs. 5 | 3 vs. 6 |
| 7 | 1 vs. 6 | 2 vs. 3 | 4 vs. 5 |
| 8 | 1 vs. 5 | 2 vs. 3 | 4 vs. 6 |
| 9 | 1 vs. 3 | 2 vs. 6 | 4 vs. 5 |
| 10 | 1 vs. 3 | 2 vs. 5 | 4 vs. 6 |
| 11 | 1 vs. 4 | 2 vs. 3 | 5 vs. 6 |
| 12 | 1 vs. 3 | 2 vs. 4 | 5 vs. 6 |
| 13 | 1 vs. 2 | 3 vs. 6 | 4 vs. 5 |
| 14 | 1 vs. 2 | 3 vs. 5 | 4 vs. 6 |
| 15 | 1 vs. 2 | 3 vs. 4 | 5 vs. 6 |

4.1.4. If every listed pairing creates a rematch, administration may use the most equitable alternative pairing available and document the reason.

4.1.5. Teams are not permitted to choose their own Swiss opponents.

### 4.2 Swiss Qualification and Final Ties

4.2.1. A team with three wins qualifies immediately; a team with three losses is eliminated.

4.2.2. Swiss pairings are based on match record, not map or round differential.

4.2.3. Swiss qualifiers are ranked using these criteria, in order:

1. Swiss record / qualification path: teams qualifying 3-0 rank ahead of teams qualifying 3-1, who rank ahead of teams qualifying 3-2.
2. Initial Swiss Stage seed.

4.2.4. If an administrative ruling changes a team's Swiss result or eligibility, the final Swiss qualification ranking is recalculated before the Playoff bracket is locked.

4.2.5. If two or more teams' final Swiss positions remain tied under the published seeding criteria, administration will compare their initial Swiss seed.

4.2.6. Administration may correct standings if a result is overturned, a forfeit is awarded, or a roster or integrity violation affects a team's eligibility.

## 5. Online Match Rules

### 5.1 Platform Requirements

5.1.1. All official matches must be played through the **FACEIT** platform and the official Green Pepper Season 2 tournament infrastructure.

5.1.2. Every player must use their own eligible account and join the correct match room with the registered account.

5.1.3. Match results, match links, brackets and standings will be tracked through the official tournament platform and/or channels designated by administration.

5.1.4. Players must follow match-room instructions and must not start, abandon or alter an official match without authorization.

5.1.5. A team that cannot access its match room must notify administration immediately and provide evidence of the issue.

### 5.2 Anti-Cheat and Player Eligibility

5.2.1. Players must use the anti-cheat software required by the designated competitive platform and any additional requirements announced by Green Pepper.

5.2.2. **FACEIT Anti-Cheat is mandatory for every player** in all official matches. Players must have it running and operational whenever required by FACEIT or tournament administration.

5.2.3. A match may not begin if a player fails to meet the applicable anti-cheat requirement. Administration may issue a forfeit if the issue is not resolved within the allowed start window.

5.2.4. Cheating, unauthorized third-party software, account sharing and attempts to bypass anti-cheat are prohibited.

5.2.5. Players with active bans that make them ineligible under the platform's rules or the event's announced eligibility policy may not participate.

5.2.6. If a player is banned or found ineligible during the tournament, administration may investigate previous results and impose penalties, including forfeits or disqualification, where justified.

### 5.3 Match Settings

5.3.1. The official server configuration takes precedence where it differs from this summary.

5.3.2. Match settings, regulation format, start money, round time, freeze time, overtime configuration and any platform-specific settings will be published before the tournament.

5.3.3. Players must not change server settings themselves.

5.3.4. Custom player models, unauthorized files or cosmetic modifications that provide an unfair advantage are prohibited.

### 5.4 Map Pool and Veto

5.4.1. The official map pool for Green Pepper Season 2 is the same seven-map pool used for Season 1:

- `de_ancient`
- `de_anubis`
- `de_cache`
- `de_dust2`
- `de_inferno`
- `de_mirage`
- `de_nuke`

5.4.2. If Valve or Counter-Strike 2 updates the competitive map pool after the tournament has started, Green Pepper's published seven-map pool will remain unchanged for the duration of the event, unless an exceptional change is required and formally announced.

5.4.3. Map vetoes must be completed through the official FACEIT match room or by the method specified by administration.

5.4.4. Unless a different process is announced, the higher-seeded team chooses whether to be Team A or Team B.

5.4.5. **BO3 veto procedure:**
1. The higher-seeded team chooses Team A or Team B.
2. Team A removes one map.
3. Team B removes one map.
4. Team A picks Map 1; Team B chooses the starting side.
5. Team B picks Map 2; Team A chooses the starting side.
6. Team A removes one map.
7. Team B removes one map.
8. The remaining map is the decider. A knife round or the server's designated side-selection method determines starting sides.

5.4.6. **BO5 veto procedure:**
1. The higher-seeded team chooses Team A or Team B.
2. Team A removes one map.
3. Team B removes one map.
4. Team A picks Map 1; Team B chooses the starting side.
5. Team B picks Map 2; Team A chooses the starting side.
6. Team A picks Map 3; Team B chooses the starting side.
7. Team B picks Map 4; Team A chooses the starting side.
8. The remaining map is the decider. A knife round or the server's designated side-selection method determines starting sides.

5.4.7. Map picks and bans are final once confirmed, except where an administrative or platform error requires correction.

5.4.8. If a team does not complete its veto within the stated deadline, administration may complete the remaining steps on its behalf.

5.4.9. The seven-map pool listed in Section 5.4.1 remains in effect for the event, subject only to an exceptional change formally announced by Green Pepper.

### 5.5 Servers and Region

5.5.1. Green Pepper Season 2 is a European event.

5.5.2. Matches use the server selected by the platform or designated by administration.

5.5.3. Players are responsible for ensuring that their internet connection, hardware and software are suitable for online competition.

5.5.4. A team must report a server-location or routing concern before the match where possible. Administration determines whether a server change is appropriate.

5.5.5. A player's poor connection alone does not automatically entitle the team to a reschedule or replay.

### 5.6 Integrity Verification and Mandatory Webcams

5.6.1. **Every player participating in official Green Pepper Season 2 matches must use an integrity webcam**, unless administration has expressly approved a written exception before the match. This requirement applies to all teams, regardless of VRS status or tournament stage.

5.6.2. Players must position the webcam as instructed so that the player's face and upper body are reasonably visible throughout the match. The camera must provide a live, continuous image and must not be a prerecorded feed, virtual camera or manipulated video.

5.6.3. Players must keep the webcam enabled and functional from the pre-match integrity check until the match has ended, including technical pauses and between maps, unless an administrator explicitly authorizes a temporary exception.

5.6.4. Players must follow official instructions for webcam setup, framing, lighting and any required camera or room checks. Green Pepper will communicate the technical procedure and any required software before the event or match.

5.6.5. If a webcam fails or disconnects, the player must immediately notify the opponent and tournament administration and pause the match when possible. Play may resume only as directed by administration. A brief technical issue does not automatically mean disqualification, but a player who cannot restore the required feed within the time allowed may be required to forfeit the map or match.

5.6.6. Deliberately disabling, obstructing, redirecting, spoofing or manipulating a webcam feed, using another person in place of the registered player, or refusing a reasonable integrity check is a serious integrity violation and may result in a forfeit, disqualification, prize forfeiture or further sanctions.

5.6.7. Administration may request screen sharing, relevant recordings, identity/account verification or additional reasonable checks where permitted by platform rules and applicable law.

5.6.8. Webcam footage and other integrity evidence will be accessed only for tournament integrity, dispute resolution or rule enforcement, and handled in accordance with applicable law and any privacy notice supplied by the Organizer. Players must follow the event's published data-handling instructions.

5.6.9. If a player has a genuine accessibility, medical, equipment or privacy concern affecting compliance, the team captain must contact administration before the match. Any exception must be expressly approved in writing; teams must not assume an exception has been granted.

## 6. Scheduling and Technical Issues

### 6.1 Match Scheduling

6.1.1. The official schedule will be published through Green Pepper's official channels and/or the designated tournament platform.

6.1.2. All displayed times use the time zone specified in the official schedule. If no time zone is stated, teams must ask administration rather than assume.

6.1.3. Teams are responsible for checking match windows, confirming match arrangements and being available during the announced period.

6.1.4. Captains must coordinate through the official match chat or designated Green Pepper communication channel.

6.1.5. A proposed time is not approved until the opponent and, where required, administration have confirmed it.

### 6.2 Rescheduling

6.2.1. Reschedule requests must be made in the official match chat or support channel and include the match link, reason and proposed alternative times.

6.2.2. Rescheduling is valid only when approved by administration and within the relevant stage's published schedule window.

6.2.3. Administration may deny a reschedule that delays the tournament, creates an unfair competitive advantage or conflicts with later matches.

6.2.4. Teams must not use rescheduling to avoid an opponent, gain unfair preparation time or delay a result.

6.2.5. If teams cannot agree on a time, they must contact administration before the deadline. Administration may set a mandatory match time.

### 6.3 Punctuality and Forfeits

6.3.1. Teams must be ready at the confirmed match start time, with all eligible players available and required anti-cheat and integrity checks functioning.

6.3.2. A **15-minute grace period** applies from the scheduled match start time. Teams must be ready to join the server and begin the match within this period.

6.3.3. If a team fails to be ready within the 15-minute grace period, it will receive a **default loss** for the match. In a BO3 match, the same sanction applies to the map in question: the team that is not ready will forfeit that map.

6.3.4. After a map forfeit in a BO3, both teams will have an additional **15 minutes** to join the server and start the next map. If the team is still not ready within that additional period, it will receive a default loss for the next map, subject to administration's review of documented exceptional circumstances.

6.3.5. A match not completed within its scheduled window without an approved reschedule may be recorded as a forfeit.

6.3.6. Administration may consider documented platform outages or exceptional circumstances before confirming a forfeit.

6.3.7. Teams must provide evidence promptly when disputing a no-show or forfeit decision.

### 6.4 Pauses and Timeouts

6.4.1. Technical pauses are for genuine technical issues such as a game crash, server problem or player disconnection. Teams must explain the reason when requested.

6.4.2. Unless the platform configuration or a published event-specific rule provides otherwise, each team may request up to one technical timeout of up to 15 minutes per match.

6.4.3. Tactical pauses are limited to the number and duration supported by the official match configuration. Captains must request pauses through the approved method.

6.4.4. Teams must not use technical pauses to gain an unfair tactical advantage, disrupt an opponent or delay a match without cause.

6.4.5. Administration may shorten, extend or deny a pause based on the circumstances and tournament schedule.

### 6.5 Disconnections and Technical Problems

6.5.1. If a player disconnects, the affected team must use the in-game pause feature where available and notify the opponent and administration as soon as possible.

6.5.2. Teams must not resume play until the issue has been addressed according to server rules and administrator instructions.

6.5.3. The response depends on match state, server capability, platform rules and whether the issue affects one or both teams.

6.5.4. A replay or rollback is not automatic. Administration decides whether a replay, continuation, round restart or other remedy is appropriate.

6.5.5. Repeated or unexplained disconnections may result in a forfeit if a team cannot complete the match.

6.5.6. If a CS2 update or platform maintenance prevents a match from running properly, teams must pause where possible and contact administration. Teams must not independently restart or reschedule an affected official match.

6.5.7. Players are responsible for their internet connection, PC, peripherals, drivers and software. A personal power, hardware or ISP issue does not automatically require a replay or reschedule.

6.5.8. Administration may grant an exception when a documented, widespread technical incident materially affects the match and a remedy is necessary to protect competitive integrity.

## 8. Penalties and Prohibited Conduct

Cheating, account sharing, stream sniping, match manipulation, harassment and other integrity violations may result in penalties up to and including disqualification. Administration considers the evidence, severity, intent, competitive impact and prior violations.

### 8.1 Cheating and Third-Party Software

8.1.1. Cheating of any kind is prohibited and may result in immediate disqualification.

8.1.2. Unauthorized third-party software, scripts, hardware or modifications that provide an unfair advantage are prohibited.

8.1.3. Account sharing, impersonation and allowing an unregistered player to compete are prohibited.

8.1.4. Attempting to bypass anti-cheat or an integrity check is a serious violation.

8.1.5. Custom models or unauthorized files that provide a competitive advantage are prohibited.

8.1.6. If a player is found to have cheated or becomes ineligible during the event, administration may review the team's results and impose forfeits or disqualification where justified.

### 8.2 Exploits and Illegal Actions

8.2.1. Deliberately exploiting a game bug for an unfair advantage is prohibited.

8.2.2. Pixel walking, use of invisible or unintended ledges and other prohibited map exploits are not allowed where they violate CS2 competitive rules or event instructions.

8.2.3. Players must not use unintended bomb-plant locations or glitches to make the objective inaccessible or unfair.

8.2.4. Players must not intentionally join the opposing team or manipulate team assignment to gain an advantage.

8.2.5. Boosting or clipping through solid geometry to gain an illegal position is prohibited.

8.2.6. Players must report a serious unintended exploit to administration rather than continue abusing it.

8.2.7. Penalties depend on the nature and impact of the violation and may include a round loss, map loss, match forfeit or disqualification.

### 8.3 Communication and Behaviour

8.3.1. Players, coaches and team representatives must treat opponents, administrators, casters and other participants respectfully.

8.3.2. Racist, discriminatory, threatening or harassing conduct is prohibited.

8.3.3. Serious discriminatory abuse may result in a match forfeit, removal from the event or a tournament ban. Repeated offences may lead to stronger penalties.

8.3.4. Threats, doxxing, targeted harassment and attempts to intimidate an opponent or administrator are serious violations.

8.3.5. Publicly sharing private administrative discussions, confidential investigation material or private personal information without authorization may result in penalties.

8.3.6. All participants must follow reasonable instructions from tournament administration and uphold fair play.

### 8.4 Stream Sniping and Outside Information

8.4.1. Players and coaches must not watch a live broadcast of their own ongoing match.

8.4.2. This restriction applies between maps and during technical pauses.

8.4.3. Teams must not receive live information from spectators, casters, friends or other outside sources.

8.4.4. Suspected stream sniping or outside communication will be investigated using available evidence.

8.4.5. Proven violations may result in a round or match loss, disqualification or a ban.

### 8.5 Betting and Match Manipulation

8.5.1. Players, coaches and team representatives must not bet on their own matches or use confidential tournament information for betting.

8.5.2. Match-fixing, intentionally losing, collusion, result manipulation or attempts to influence another team to manipulate a match are prohibited.

8.5.3. Participants must report credible approaches involving match manipulation to tournament administration.

8.5.4. Violations may result in immediate disqualification, prize forfeiture and referral to the relevant platform or competition integrity body where appropriate.

### 8.6 Repeated Violations and Sanctions

8.6.1. Repeated violations may result in escalating penalties.

8.6.2. Administration may remove a team whose conduct repeatedly disrupts the event or undermines competitive integrity.

8.6.3. A team is responsible for the conduct of its players, coach and representatives participating in event activities.

8.6.4. Penalties may include a warning, round loss, map loss, match forfeit, disqualification, prize forfeiture or exclusion from future Green Pepper events.

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do all players need a FACEIT account and FACEIT Anti-Cheat?**  
A: Yes. Every player must use their own eligible FACEIT account and have FACEIT Anti-Cheat running for official matches.

**Q: How does direct Playoff qualification work in Season 2?**  
A: The eight highest-ranked eligible European VRS teams among the accepted entrants qualify directly for the 16-team Playoffs, using the official **2 November 2026 European VRS snapshot** and subject to the roster and eligibility requirements in this Rulebook.

**Q: What happens to teams that do not receive a direct Playoff place?**  
A: The remaining 16 teams compete in the BO3 Swiss Stage. A team qualifies after three wins and is eliminated after three losses. The eight Swiss qualifiers join the eight VRS direct qualifiers in the 16-team Playoffs.

**Q: How are Swiss Stage matches played?**  
A: All Swiss matches are best-of-three (BO3). Teams are paired against opponents with the same current win-loss record, avoiding rematches where a valid alternative is available.

**Q: What is the Playoff format?**  
A: The 16-team Playoffs use a Single-Elimination bracket. All matches are BO3 except the Grand Final, which is BO5.

**Q: Can multiple teams from the same esports organization participate?**  
A: No. Only one team per organization may participate in Green Pepper Season 2. Teams must disclose organizational affiliations, and administration may reject or disqualify teams where an organizational conflict is confirmed.

**Q: What counts as a team's core?**  
A: For a VRS-matched team, the reference roster is the five players on its matched VRS roster in the applicable 2 November 2026 snapshot. For a non-VRS team, it is the five original registered starters. At least three players from the applicable reference roster must play the entirety of every official match.

**Q: Can we change our roster?**  
A: A maximum of two player changes in total is permitted after roster submission, subject to the time windows, VRS-core protections and administration approval in Section 2.4.

**Q: Does using an already registered substitute count as a roster change?**  
A: Simply fielding a substitute already listed on the approved roster does not count as a roster change. Every lineup must still satisfy the applicable three-of-five requirement.

**Q: Can a player be substituted during a match?**  
A: Only in a verifiable emergency and subject to administration approval.

**Q: What happens if a team is late for a match?**  
A: A 15-minute grace period applies. Failure to be ready results in a default loss for the match or, in a BO3, the map in question. After a map forfeit, teams have an additional 15 minutes to start the next map.

**Q: What if a player disconnects?**  
A: Pause where the platform allows, notify the opponent and administration immediately, and follow their instructions. A replay is not automatic.

**Q: Can I stream my POV or cast a match?**  
A: Player POV streaming is permitted only if administration is notified in advance and the mandatory 120-second broadcast delay is applied. Community broadcasting and co-streaming require prior approval from Green Pepper.

**Q: How do I submit a protest?**  
A: Use the official Green Pepper support channel and submit the match details and supporting evidence. The standard deadline is 24 hours after the match concludes unless a different deadline is announced.

**Q: Will registration fees be refunded if the tournament is cancelled, does not reach 24 accepted teams or does not obtain its intended VRS status/alignment?**  
A: Yes. The refund conditions in Section 1.3 apply. A team that voluntarily withdraws after its payment has been confirmed is not entitled to a refund, subject to the terms of this Rulebook and applicable law.

**Q: Who makes the final decision on an issue not covered here?**  
A: Green Pepper tournament administration, applying the principles of competitive integrity, consistency and fairness.
